  tests

fixtures, not defects.** They are fixed.

## 5. Design decisions you made

Recorded so they are not relitigated:

- **`graph` enum over booleans.** You rejected a two-boolean proposal;
  the enum makes `hierarchy: true, acyclic: false` *unspellable* rather
  than validated-against.
- **Cycles reaching a tree axis** — `buildTree` reports them itself;
  tree view renders the acyclic part with a banner; tightening `graph`
  warns rather than blocks.
- **Status default is explicit config**, not positional. Exactly one
  `default: true`; the default cannot be deleted.
- **`n` only** for the create shortcut; `c` dropped.
- **Project column** is scope-driven, overriding `list_columns`.
- **Body save** is a conditional write carrying a base version.
- **Bulk selection**: cleared on full success, failures retained.
- **No ownership checks on comments** — LocTT has no roles; `editors`
  is a provenance trail, not a permission record.
- **Mockups are a design reference, not scaffolding.** All 39 app
  design tokens originate in `temp-ui-mockups/tokens.css`.
